## Sample documents

The files under `samples/` were made for this project by `samples/make_samples.py`.

- Their package skeleton (styles and settings of an empty document) comes from the blank document of [python-hwpx](https://github.com/airmang/python-hwpx), Copyright 2025-2026 airmang, licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). Changes: text, a generated picture and C2PA Content Credentials were added; `version.xml` names the generator.
- The files `3a-…` and `3b-…` were then opened, edited and saved with Hangul 12.30 for macOS, which rewrote the package.
- The picture is generated by the script. No fonts are embedded; the documents only name fonts.
- The certificates are test certificates created for these samples. Only the root certificate is published; they give no assurance about anything.
